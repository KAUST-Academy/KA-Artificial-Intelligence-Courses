\begin{frame}{Summary: Three Threads}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.45em
    \item \textbf{RLHF (the focus).} When reward is hard to specify, learn it:
          \textbf{SFT $\rightarrow$ RM $\rightarrow$ PPO/GRPO}. Comparisons become a
          loss through \textbf{Bradley--Terry}; the KL leash exists because the RM is
          a \emph{proxy} (reward hacking). Solving ``reward minus $\beta$-KL'' gives
          $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ tilted by $e^{\,r/\beta}$, and reading that tilt
          backwards is \textbf{DPO}: no RM, no RL loop, the same
          $-\log\sigma(\Delta)$ loss. The frontier climbs the oversight spectrum:
          \textbf{RLHF $\rightarrow$ RLAIF $\rightarrow$ RLVR} (verifiable rewards,
          how reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1 are trained).
    \item \textbf{Multi-Agent RL.} The environment is \emph{non-stationary} as
          others learn; \textbf{CTDE} is the dominant fix; \textbf{self-play}
          builds an automatic curriculum.
    \item \textbf{Robotics.} Sample cost, safety, resets, and the reality gap;
          \textbf{domain randomization} and max-entropy methods (SAC $\to$ SERL)
          make real-hardware RL practical.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
