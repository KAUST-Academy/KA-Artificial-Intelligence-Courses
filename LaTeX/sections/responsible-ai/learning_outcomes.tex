\section{Learning Outcomes}
% =========================
% 1. Overview
% =========================

\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    \begin{itemize}
        \setlength\itemsep{0.7em}
        \item Identify where bias enters an ML system --- data collection, labels, proxy variables, historical patterns, and feedback loops --- and audit a model with disaggregated metrics.

        \item State the main group fairness definitions and explain why they cannot all hold at the same time.

        \item Apply SHAP and LIME to a trained model, and describe their assumptions and failure modes.

        \item Write a model card and a datasheet for a model you are shipping.

        \item Summarise what current regulation and frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF) ask of a practitioner.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
