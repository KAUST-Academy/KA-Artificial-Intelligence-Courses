\section{Motivation}
\begin{frame}{Motivation}
    \large Why this topic matters:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Transformers dominate NLP --- can the same idea work for images?
            \item Vision Transformers (ViTs) treat an image as a sequence of patches.
            \item With enough data, ViTs match or beat CNNs on many benchmarks.
            \item Hierarchical and hybrid designs make them practical for detection and segmentation.
            \item \textit{"An image is worth 16x16 words."} --- \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929}{Dosovitskiy et al., 2020}.
        \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
