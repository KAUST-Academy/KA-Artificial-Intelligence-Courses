\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Normalizing Flow Models - Summary}
\textbf{Key Takeaways:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Normalizing flows model complex distributions while keeping likelihoods exact.
    \item They transform simple base distributions into complex data distributions using invertible mappings.
    \item The change of variables formula and the Jacobian determinant are central to their mathematical foundation.
    \item Architectures such as NICE, RealNVP, and Glow illustrate the evolution and increasing sophistication of flow-based models.
    \item Flow matching revives flows in continuous time: train a velocity field by regression on noise-data interpolations, then sample by integrating an ODE; it powers current state-of-the-art image, video, and speech generators.
\end{itemize}
\framebreak

\textbf{Considerations:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item There are trade-offs between model expressiveness and computational efficiency.
    \item Efficient learning and density estimation both depend on designing transformations with tractable Jacobians.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
