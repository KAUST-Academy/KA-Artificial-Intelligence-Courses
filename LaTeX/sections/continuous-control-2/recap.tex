\section{Recap}
\begin{frame}{}
    \LARGE Soft Actor-Critic: \textbf{Recap (Day 5)}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Recap: Where Day 5 Left Us}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Day 5 gave us \textbf{off-policy continuous control}: DDPG \rref{4} / TD3 \rref{5} --- a replay buffer, target networks, and a deterministic actor $\mu_\theta(s)$
    \pause
    \item But DDPG is \textbf{fragile}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item deterministic actor $\Rightarrow$ \emph{no native exploration} --- Gaussian noise has to be bolted on by hand, with its own scale and schedule to tune
        \item brittle to hyperparameters and random seeds
        \item mode collapse on multimodal $Q$-landscapes --- commits to one of several equally good actions
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Recap: What Today Delivers}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Today --- Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)} \rref{1}: fix all three with a \emph{stochastic} actor and an \emph{entropy bonus} in the objective
    $$J(\pi) = \sum_t \mathbb{E}_{(s_t,a_t)\sim\rho_\pi}\big[\, r(s_t,a_t) + \alpha\,\mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot\mid s_t)) \,\big]$$
    \item Entropy drives \emph{directed} exploration --- no hand-tuned noise schedule; robust to seeds; captures multimodal optima
    \item \textbf{Everything from Day 5 carries over:} replay buffer, target networks, and TD3's clipped double-Q critic
    \item We close with \textbf{automatic temperature tuning} (SAC v2) --- removing the last brittle hyperparameter
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
