\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Explain why language needs grounding, and map the vision-language task space to its canonical
      benchmarks.
    \item Explain contrastive vision-language alignment (CLIP, SigLIP) and what dual encoders can and
      cannot do.
    \item Describe visual instruction tuning, the projector recipe behind multimodal assistants, and how
      images become tokens.
    \item Survey the 2025--2026 generation: open-weight flagships, natively multimodal frontier systems,
      unified understanding-and-generation models, and omni models.
    \item Explain the applications beyond chat: video, documents, multimodal retrieval, GUI agents, and
      vision-language-action robotics.
    \item Interpret multimodal benchmarks (MMMU, MMMU-Pro) and identify standing failure modes such as
      object hallucination, visual token cost, and multimodal jailbreaks.
    \item Compare early, middle and late fusion, and the classic challenges of multimodal integration.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
