\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.7\textwidth}
\begin{itemize}
    \item DDPG = DQN + DPG (Lillicrap et al., 2015)
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \underline{off-policy} \underline{actor-critic} method that learns a \underline{deterministic} policy in \underline{continuous} domain
        \item exploration noise added to the deterministic policy when select action
        \vspace{0.4em}
        \item difficult to stabilize and brittle to hyperparameters (Duan et al., 2016, Henderson et al., 2017)
        \item unscalable to complex tasks with high dimensions (Gu et al., 2017)
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
