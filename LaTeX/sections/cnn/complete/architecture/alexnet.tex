\subsection{AlexNet}
\begin{frame}{}
    \LARGE CNN Architectures: \textbf{AlexNet}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{AlexNet}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item First big improvement in image classification: won ILSVRC 2012 with 16.4\% top-5 error, down from 25.8\% the year before.
        \item Made use of convolutional layers, pooling, dropout, ReLU and training on GPUs.
        \item 5 convolutional layers, some followed by max-pooling layers, with three fully connected layers at the end.

    \end{itemize}
