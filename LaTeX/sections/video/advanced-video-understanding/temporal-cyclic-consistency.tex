\subsection{Temporal cycle consistency}
\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Temporal cycle consistency}
    \textbf{Temporal Cycle Consistency} is a self-supervised learning approach that uses the temporal structure of video itself as supervision: correct correspondences form cycles that return to where they started.

    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Cycle Consistency:} Match a patch or frame forward to another point in time and back again; the training loss penalizes cycles that fail to return to the starting frame.
        \item \textbf{Across Videos:} The same idea aligns two videos of the same action, matching each frame to its nearest neighbour in the other video's embedding space and back.
        \item \textbf{Applications:} Video alignment, tracking, and correspondence learning.
    \end{itemize}
