\section{Learning Outcomes}
\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    By the end of this session, you should be able to:
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Explain the core idea of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures and why they decouple model capacity from per-token compute.
        \item Contrast sparse and dense models and reason about their trade-offs.
        \item Describe gating mechanisms, top-$k$ routing, and noisy gating.
        \item Explain load balancing, the auxiliary loss, and expert-collapse failure modes.
        \item Identify how MoE layers integrate into Transformers (Switch Transformer, GShard, Mixtral, DeepSeek) and how expert parallelism distributes them.
        \item Assess the benefits and limitations of MoE models in practice.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
