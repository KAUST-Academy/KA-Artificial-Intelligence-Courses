\begin{frame}{GAE: The Two Limits}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The single knob $\lambda$ in $\hat{A}^{\mathrm{GAE}(\gamma,\lambda)}_t = \sum_{l \geq 0} (\gamma\lambda)^l\, \delta_{t+l}$ interpolates the bias--variance trade-off:
    \item $\lambda = 0$: pure 1-step TD --- low variance, biased
    \item $\lambda = 1$: full Monte Carlo ($R_t - V_\phi(s_t)$) --- unbiased, high variance
    \item $\lambda \approx 0.95$: standard in PPO implementations
    \item \textbf{Read $\lambda$ as ``how much do I trust my critic?''} --- small $\lambda$ leans on $V_\phi$, large $\lambda$ leans on the observed rewards
    \pause
    \item \textbf{Computed backwards in one pass} over a rollout --- the form you will implement:
    $$\hat{A}_t = \delta_t + \gamma\lambda\, \hat{A}_{t+1}, \qquad \hat{A}_T = 0$$
    \item We write $\hat{A}_t$ for short: our \textbf{practical estimate} of the true advantage $A^{\pi}(s_t,a_t)$
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
