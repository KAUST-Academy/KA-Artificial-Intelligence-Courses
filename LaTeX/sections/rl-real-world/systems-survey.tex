\begin{frame}{RL Also Runs Systems}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.52\textwidth}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Illustrative example: chip floorplanning} (Mirhoseini et al.,
\emph{Nature} 2021; later named \textbf{AlphaChip}):
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.25em
    \item Place macros on a chip canvas to minimize wirelength, congestion,
          and density.
    \item The striking idea: \emph{pre-train} a placement policy on many
          netlists, then place a \emph{new} chip in a forward pass, instead
          of optimizing each one from scratch.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.4em}
\textbf{Also deployed for:} datacenter resource scheduling, compiler / device
placement, real-time ad bidding.
\end{column}
