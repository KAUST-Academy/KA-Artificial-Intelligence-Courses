\subsection{Learning the Speediness in Videos}
\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Learning the Speediness in Videos}
    \textbf{Learning Speediness} is a self-supervised learning approach that focuses on understanding the temporal dynamics of videos by learning to recognize whether a video is played at its natural speed.

    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Pretext Task:} A binary classifier predicts whether a clip plays at normal speed or artificially sped up, which forces the network to attend to motion cues.
        \item \textbf{Applications:} Useful for tasks like action recognition, video summarization, and adaptive video speedup.
    \end{itemize}
\framebreak
    \textbf{Ultimate Goal:} Watch video content faster by adaptively speeding up the video
