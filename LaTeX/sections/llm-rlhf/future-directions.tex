\begin{frame}{Future Directions}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{RLAIF (RL from AI Feedback):} Replace the human preference labeler with an off-the-shelf LLM, so preference data can be generated at scale (Bai et al., 2022).
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Constitutional AI:} A written list of principles drives two stages: the model first critiques and revises its own responses, then AI-labeled preferences supply the RLHF reward signal (Bai et al., 2022).
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Open RLHF Datasets:} Promote better community sharing and reproducibility with open preference datasets.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Multi-modal Alignment:} Extend alignment techniques to vision, audio, and code generation tasks.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Reward Hacking Defense:} Build reward models that hold up under heavy optimization, since gold-standard quality starts to fall once the proxy reward is pushed too far (Gao et al., 2023).
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
