\begin{frame}{What's Next: Day 6}
\begin{itemize}
    \item DDPG and TD3 give us \emph{off-policy, continuous control} --- but with a \textbf{deterministic} policy and only weak, injected exploration
    \pause
    \item \textbf{Day 6 --- Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)} \rref{5}\textbf{:} maximum-entropy RL
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Stochastic policy with an explicit \textbf{entropy bonus}: $\mathcal{J}(\pi_\theta) = \mathbb{E}\big[\sum_t r_t + \alpha\,\mathcal{H}(\pi_\theta(\cdot|s_t))\big]$ --- PPO's $c_2\mathcal{H}$ term promoted to a first-class citizen
        \item Entropy drives \emph{directed} exploration --- no hand-tuned noise schedule
        \item Reuses today's machinery: replay buffer, target networks, \textbf{TD3's clipped double-Q}
        \item The deterministic policy gradient $\nabla_\theta Q_\phi(s,\mu_\theta(s))$ generalises to the \textbf{reparameterised} stochastic gradient --- the noise returns, now differentiable
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Day 7 --- Exploration:} injected action noise is today's weakest part. \emph{Hindsight Experience Replay} \rref{7} --- relabelling failed rollouts with the goal they \emph{did} reach --- is the standard companion to DDPG/TD3 in sparse-reward robotics
    \item Takeaway: SAC is the entropy-regularised, stochastic sibling of TD3 --- today's machinery carries over
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
