\section{Learning Outcomes}
\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    \begin{itemize}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.8em}
        \item Explain why $\arg\max_a Q(s,a)$ is intractable in continuous action spaces, motivating a learned deterministic actor
        \item Derive the deterministic policy gradient from the stochastic policy gradient and identify the $\nabla_a Q$ term
        \item Describe how DDPG combines DPG with the DQN tricks (replay buffer, target networks) into an off-policy actor-critic
        \item Explain the role of soft target updates, batch normalisation, and added exploration noise (OU vs.\ Gaussian)
        \item State DDPG's failure modes (Q-overestimation, brittleness) and how TD3's three fixes address them
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
