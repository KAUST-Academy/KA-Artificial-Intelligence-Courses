\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Agentic AI} adds goals, memory, and tools to a
      language model. \textbf{Function calling} is the mechanism; \textbf{MCP} standardises the interface.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{The first autonomous frameworks (Auto-GPT, BabyAGI)}
      established the loop but performed poorly on real tasks. Their components survived; the assumption of
      full autonomy did not.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Code generation} is the agent's strongest
      actuator: executability gives cheap, trustworthy feedback --- unit-test evaluation (pass@k,
      SWE-bench), execution-driven repair, and verifiable rewards all flow from it.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Continual learning} remains open: agents must absorb
      new tasks without overwriting old ones.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Agency turns a wrong answer into a wrong action, which is why
      safety and oversight get their own treatment next.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
