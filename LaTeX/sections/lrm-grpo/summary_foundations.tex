\begin{frame}{Summary}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.45em
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Large reasoning models} differ from pattern-matching LLMs: they spend test-time compute on explicit multi-step reasoning traces.
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Prompting techniques} (Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thought, Self-Consistency) elicit reasoning from capable models without any training.
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Instruction tuning} (FLAN and successors) teaches models to follow task descriptions and generalize to unseen tasks; CoT fine-tuning bakes reasoning into the weights.
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] The leading systems (o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, Gemini Flash Thinking, QwQ) combine these ingredients with RL post-training.
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Costs remain: verbose traces, prompt sensitivity, and inference compute overhead.
      \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{A trace is not a proof.} Models can write reasoning that omits what actually drove the answer, so verify the answer rather than trusting the prose.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
