\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
  By the end of this lecture you should be able to:
  \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.5em
    \item[\textcolor{KAred}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Explain why giving an LLM the ability to act changes the safety problem, and classify agent failures as misuse, accidents, misalignment, or adversarial.
    \item[\textcolor{KAred}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Describe the agent-specific risks: excessive agency, long-horizon error compounding, irreversible actions, and deceptive behavior under evaluation.
    \item[\textcolor{KAred}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Explain direct and indirect prompt injection, why the model does not separate data from instructions, and what the lethal trifecta is.
    \item[\textcolor{KAred}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Apply system-level and model-level safeguards, and state their limits.
    \item[\textcolor{KAred}{$\blacktriangleright$}] Reason about multi-agent trust boundaries and place agent safety inside the OWASP and NIST governance frameworks.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
