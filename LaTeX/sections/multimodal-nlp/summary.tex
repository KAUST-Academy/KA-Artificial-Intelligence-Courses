\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Contrastive alignment} (CLIP, then SigLIP) gives
      encoders that score and retrieve; conversation took a decoder LLM behind a projector plus visual
      instruction tuning.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{The open recipe scaled} through LLaVA's successors
      into today's flagships (Qwen3-VL, InternVL3.5): native resolution, grounding, documents, video.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Frontier systems are natively multimodal:} one
      network reads and writes text, images and audio (GPT-4o onward), reasons by acting on images (o3),
      and generates images mid-conversation (Nano Banana).
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{The same perception stack works beyond chat:}
      retrieving page images, driving GUIs, controlling robots.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Where fusion happens} (early, middle, late) is
      still the design axis; at scale, early fusion concedes nothing.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Benchmarks brush the human-expert ceiling}
      (MMMU-Pro in the low-80s); hallucination, token cost, jailbreaks and fragile evaluation stay open.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] The agentic systems built on these models, their loops,
      tools and safety questions, are covered later in the course.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
