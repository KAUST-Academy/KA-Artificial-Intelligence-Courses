\section{Learning Outcomes}
\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    \begin{itemize}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.7em}
        \item Frame ``the reward problem'' and distinguish its three faces:
              \emph{exploration vs.\ exploitation}, \emph{reward design}, and \emph{reward learning}
        \item State the multi-armed bandit problem and \emph{regret}; explain why
              $\epsilon$-greedy is provably wasteful while UCB and Thompson sampling are not
        \item Scale these ideas to deep RL --- \emph{bootstrapped DQN as posterior
              sampling}, pseudo-counts, ICM, RND, and the episodic-vs-lifelong novelty of
              NGU / Agent57 / Go-Explore --- and recognise UCB's $1/\sqrt{N}$ bonus wherever it reappears
        \item Recognise the \emph{same} dilemma in today's LLM training: entropy collapse
              and pass@k in RL for reasoning models
        \item Motivate \emph{inverse RL} and \emph{preference-based reward learning}, and
              trace the line from MaxEnt IRL through adversarial IRL to the reward model
              in RLHF on Day 9
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
