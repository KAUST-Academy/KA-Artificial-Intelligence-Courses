% Slide 2
\begin{frame}{Recap}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Sometimes, it is better to learn a stochastic policy than a
          deterministic policy
    \item Policy gradient methods suffer from poor sample efficiency
    \item Importance sampling can help alleviate this problem
    \item However, we need to make sure that current policy is not far from
          policy with which we have collected trajectories
    \item Small changes in the policy parameters can unexpectedly lead to big
          changes in the policy.
    \item TRPO uses importance sampling to take multiple gradient steps and
          constrains the optimization objective in the policy space
    \item PPO does the same but approximately enforces KL constraint
          without computing natural gradients.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
