\subsection{Self-Supervision in Videos}
\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Self-Supervision in Videos}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Temporal Order:} Learning to predict or verify the correct temporal sequence of video frames as a self-supervised task.
        \item \textbf{Learning Correspondence:} Matching patches across frames, learned from unlabeled video, as a signal for tracking and segmentation.
        \item \textbf{Cycle Consistency:} Enforcing that matches made forward in time can be traced back to their starting point, ensuring consistency in learned representations.
        \item \textbf{Video Speedup:} Training models to recognize videos played at different speeds, encouraging temporal understanding.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
