\section{Motivation}
\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Motivation}
\noindent
\textbf{Three departures from the standard recipe of an autoregressive transformer over subword tokens:}
\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Open-weight LLMs:} Releases like the LLaMA 3 herd bring frontier-level models into the open, widening access to research and deployment.
    \item \textbf{Tokenizer-free modeling:} the Byte Latent Transformer (BLT) replaces subword tokenization with learned byte patches.
    \item \textbf{Non-autoregressive generation:} Large Language Diffusion Models (LLDMs) generate text by iterative denoising instead of left-to-right prediction.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
