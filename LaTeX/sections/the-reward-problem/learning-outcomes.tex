\section{Learning Outcomes}
\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    \begin{itemize}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.8em}
        \item Frame ``the reward problem'' and distinguish its three faces:
              \emph{exploration vs.\ exploitation}, \emph{reward design}, and \emph{reward learning}
        \item State the multi-armed bandit problem, regret, and the Lai-Robbins
              lower bound; \emph{derive} UCB1 from Hoeffding's inequality
        \item Place $\epsilon$-greedy, UCB, Thompson sampling, and gradient bandits
              on a single map of bandit exploration strategies
        \item Scale these ideas to deep RL: \emph{bootstrapped DQN as approximate
              posterior sampling (PSRL)}, pseudo-counts, ICM, and RND ---
              the answer for the hard-exploration tasks SAC's entropy bonus struggles on
        \item Motivate \emph{inverse RL} and \emph{preference-based reward learning}
              as the conceptual ancestor of RLHF on Day 9
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
