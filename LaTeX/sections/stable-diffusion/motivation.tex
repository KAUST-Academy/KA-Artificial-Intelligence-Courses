\begin{frame}{Where We Left Off}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.4em
        \item GANs and VAEs made image synthesis work, but each pays for it: GANs with unstable training and mode collapse, VAEs with blurry samples.
        \item Diffusion models replace one hard optimisation with many easy ones. The objective is a simple regression onto the added noise, training is stable, and sample quality is state of the art.
        \item The price is \textbf{iterative sampling}: hundreds of network evaluations per image. Most of this lecture is about paying that price down, by moving to a latent space, by better samplers, and by distillation.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
