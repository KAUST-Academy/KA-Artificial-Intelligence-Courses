\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
After this session, you will be able to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Describe cascaded diffusion pipelines for high-resolution generation.
    \item Summarize score-based diffusion models and their applications (DreamFusion, text-to-SVG, video adapters).
    \item Explain how diffusion models can be used to solve PDEs (DiffusionPDE).
    \item Utilize text-conditioned diffusion models for controlled image generation.
    \item Explain CLIP-guided generation and editing with VQ-GAN and StyleCLIP.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
