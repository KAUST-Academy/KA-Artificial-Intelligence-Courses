\begin{frame}{What's Next: Day 6}
\begin{itemize}
    \item DDPG and TD3 give us \emph{off-policy, continuous control} --- but with a \textbf{deterministic} policy and only weak, injected exploration
    \pause
    \item \textbf{Day 6 --- Soft Actor-Critic (SAC):} maximum-entropy RL
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Stochastic policy with an explicit \textbf{entropy bonus} in the objective: $\mathcal{J}(\pi) = \mathbb{E}\big[\sum_t r_t + \alpha\,\mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot|s_t))\big]$
        \item Entropy drives \emph{directed} exploration --- no hand-tuned OU/Gaussian noise schedule
        \item Reuses what we built today: replay buffer, target networks, and \textbf{TD3's clipped double-Q} critic
        \item The deterministic policy gradient $\nabla_\theta Q(s,\mu_\theta(s))$ generalises to the \textbf{reparameterised} stochastic gradient
    \end{itemize}
    \item Takeaway: SAC is the entropy-regularised, stochastic sibling of TD3 --- everything from today carries over
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
