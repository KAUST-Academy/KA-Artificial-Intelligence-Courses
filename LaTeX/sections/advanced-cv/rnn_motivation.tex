\section{Motivation}
\begin{frame}{Motivation}
    \large Why this topic matters:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Many real-world signals are sequential: text, speech, video, and time series.
            \item Standard CNNs and MLPs expect fixed-size inputs and have no memory across time steps.
            \item RNNs process variable-length sequences while sharing weights across time.
            \item They are the foundation for sequence-to-sequence learning and the attention mechanism.
            \item Their failure modes are what the architectures covered later are built to avoid.
        \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
