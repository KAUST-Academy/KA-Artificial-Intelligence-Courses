\section{Learning Outcomes}
\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    \begin{itemize}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.8em}
        \item Explain why you cannot take $\arg\max_a Q(s,a)$ over a continuous action space, and why that forces us to \emph{learn} a deterministic actor $\mu_\theta$
        \item Explain why the Days 3--4 policy gradient stops working for a deterministic actor, and state the rule that replaces it: the critic supplies a \emph{direction} $\nabla_a Q_\phi$ rather than a score
        \item Say what changes relative to Day 4's actor-critic: actor $\pi_\theta \to \mu_\theta$, critic $V_\phi(s) \to Q_\phi(s,a)$, on-policy rollouts $\to$ replay buffer
        \item Read the DDPG algorithm and locate the replay buffer, the target networks, the soft update, the exploration noise, and the two update rules
        \item Explain why DDPG's TD target needs no $\max_{a'}$, and what soft target updates and batch normalisation contribute to stability
        \item State DDPG's failure modes (Q-overestimation, brittleness, weak exploration) and how TD3's three fixes address them
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
