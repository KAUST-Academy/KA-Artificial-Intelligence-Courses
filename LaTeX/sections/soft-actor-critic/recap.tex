\section{Recap \& References}
\begin{frame}{Recap: SAC}
\begin{itemize}
    \item An off-policy maximum entropy deep reinforcement learning algorithm
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Sample-efficient
        \item Scale to high-dimensional observation/action space
        \item Robustness to random seed, noise and etc.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Theoretical Results
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Convergence of soft policy iteration
        \item Derivation of soft-actor critic algorithm
    \end{itemize}
    \item Empirical Results
    \begin{itemize}
        \item SAC outperforms SOTA model-free deep RL methods, including DDPG, PPO and Soft Q-learning, in terms of the policy's optimality, sample complexity and robustness.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
