\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}\footnotesize
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Inference optimisation lowers latency, increases throughput, and reduces memory.}
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{KV caching} avoids recomputing past keys/values but grows memory with layers, batch, and length, motivating KV quantization, grouped attention, and paging.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Serving:} continuous batching keeps the GPU full; PagedAttention removes KV cache fragmentation; speculative decoding buys roughly 2--3$\times$ decode speed while preserving the target model's output distribution.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Distillation:} train a compact \textbf{student} to mimic a \textbf{teacher} for deployment; \textbf{soft targets} at raised \textbf{temperature} carry knowledge beyond one-hot labels.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Pruning:} remove low-value weights (unstructured, structured, or 2:4 semi-structured) and retrain; composes with the other compression techniques.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{Quantization:} shrink \textbf{weights}, \textbf{activations}, and the \textbf{KV cache} via lower-bit formats.
    \item[\textcolor{red}{$\blacktriangleright$}] \textbf{TurboQuant:} random rotation plus a per-coordinate scalar quantizer, with one bit of QJL residual to keep attention scores unbiased. It compresses the \textbf{KV cache} and search-index vectors rather than model weights, with a proved near-optimal distortion bound and no calibration data.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
