\begin{frame}{Recap: Where World Models Have Already Appeared}
  \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.3em
    \item \textbf{Covered earlier:} a world model as a \emph{predictor in representation space}; multistep and hierarchical world models; V-JEPA 2-AC planning with CEM over imagined latent futures.
    \item \textbf{Also covered earlier:} the large pretrained backbones (DINOv2, V-JEPA 2) that such systems build on.
    \item \textbf{Today, the control side of the story:} agents that learn a model of their environment, imagine futures inside it, and use those imagined futures to plan or to train a policy. This is \textbf{model-based reinforcement learning}.
    \item Route: Ha \& Schmidhuber (2018) $\rightarrow$ PlaNet / RSSM $\rightarrow$ Dreamer v1--v3 $\rightarrow$ generative simulators vs.\ JEPA $\rightarrow$ LeWorldModel.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
