    & parameterized tractable policy (the \textbf{actor})
    & Gaussian, $\mu_\theta$ and $\sigma_\theta$ from a network \\

    & critic objective \& its stochastic gradient
    & minimize soft Bellman residual \\

    & actor objective \& its stochastic gradient
    & KL to $\exp(Q/\alpha)$, reparameterized \\

\vspace{0.4em}
{\footnotesize \textcolor{red}{This is where we finally pick $\Pi$:} the actor's family is the \textbf{Gaussians} --- one mean and one spread per action dimension. That is the concrete choice policy improvement was left waiting on. Otherwise these are the same two networks as DDPG (Day 5): the critic now carries the entropy term, and the actor outputs a \emph{distribution} $(\mu_\theta,\sigma_\theta)$ instead of a single action $\mu_\theta$.}
